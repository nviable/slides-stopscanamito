Source. Amito toolkit, Oct 2026. JPEG Trust is ISO/IEC 21617-1:2026.

  • Three parts. People talk to Amito in a chat, on the web, Telegram or Discord. They upload an image or paste an Instagram or X post link, and a link brings the caption and the account with the photo.
  • Beat 2. A language model is the agent. It runs one check at a time and explains it, and the person can ask for a check by name. The model is a choice. OpenAI, Claude, or a local model on the organisation's own computer, so images need not leave it.
  • The detection service holds the methods. Provenance, the pixel detectors, the context checks, and for a link, the caption and the account.
  • Beat 3. Results come back as explained cards, an evidence board with a tip for each fact, and the JPEG Trust report when the person asks for it.
