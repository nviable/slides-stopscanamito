Source. Amito toolkit, Oct 2026.

  • Amito walks the person through STOP&SCAN in a chat, on the web, Telegram or Discord. It is built by the AMAS technology group.
  • There are two ways in. Upload the image, and Amito reads the file itself, its Content Credentials and its camera metadata.
  • Beat 2. Or paste the link of an Instagram or X post, the way people actually meet images. Amito fetches the full photo, the caption and who posted it. Bluesky, Pinterest, Flickr and Imgur links work as well.
  • Beat 3. The chat follows the five steps. At Stop, Amito asks before it checks anything, so the person's first reading comes first. Source, Content and Alignment are where Amito runs the methods. At Now Reflect, the person weighs the evidence board and asks for the report when they are ready.
