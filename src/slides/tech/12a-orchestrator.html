Sources. Amito toolkit and its trust profile (amito-default), test runs 6 to 10 Oct 2026, checked on the JPEG Trust committee's profile evaluator. JPEG Trust is ISO/IEC 21617-1:2026.

  • Amito is an agent in a chat. It doesn't run a fixed pipeline. It reads the conversation and picks one check at a time, explains it, and proposes the next. The person can also ask for a check by name, like who posted it.
  • Beat 2. Around it are the tools, coloured by the STOP&SCAN step they serve. Source in cyan, Content in green, Alignment in pink.
  • Beat 3. One route through them. Credentials first, then the pixels, then the caption. Every result goes into one trust report, where a trust profile checks its rules in order, strongest evidence first, so a signed declaration outweighs a detector score.
  • The profile is written in the JPEG Trust profile format, with our own extensions, and its statements give the same results on the committee's profile evaluator. It follows the model. We don't claim conformance. Part 2, the Trust Profiles Catalogue, will define the conformance points for profiles, and it is still in development.
