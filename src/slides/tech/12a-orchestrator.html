Sources. Amito toolkit and its trust profile (amito-default), test runs 6 to 8 Oct 2026. ISO/IEC 21617-1:2026.

  • Amito is an agent that orchestrates separate tools. It runs one check per step, explains it, and proposes the next one. The person can also ask for a check by name.
  • Each tool sits under the STOP&SCAN step it serves. Provenance, metadata, the account and the caption feed Source. The detectors feed Content. Place, weather and the story feed Alignment.
  • Beat 2. Everything goes into one JPEG Trust report. A trust profile checks its rules in order, strongest evidence first, so a signed declaration outweighs a detector score.
