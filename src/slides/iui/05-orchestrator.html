Source. AMITO, Oct 2026.

  • About 30 seconds.
  • AMITO is an agent in a chat, with no fixed pipeline and no fixed order. The LLM sees the image, picks the next check and says why. Provenance is cheap and often decisive. The location check helps once a place is claimed. The weather check needs GPS and a capture time. The account check needs a linked post.
  • Beat 2. The checks cover three levels. Provenance, which reads Content Credentials, AI labels and camera metadata, and finds earlier copies with a reverse image search. Pixels, where five AI-image detectors are fused into one calibrated probability, a face-forgery model scores the largest face, and a vision model can point out odd hands, text or shadows. And context, the place from the scenery and landmarks against the GPS, the weather on the day, and the caption and account behind a post. The colours are the STOP&SCAN steps each level serves.
  • Like an open-source investigation, AMITO brings together sources a layperson, and often a busy journalist, would rarely combine. The LLM links them to each other and to the claim, and names what would settle a discrepancy.
  • Beat 3. One route. Credentials first, then the pixels, then the caption, then the claim against the file. Every result goes into a trust report, where a written trust profile sets the reference verdict. The rules decide, not the LLM, and the person decides at Now Reflect. A fourth level, a world model, is planned.
