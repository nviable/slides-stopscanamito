C2 Joint investigation

Either the person or the agent can lead

The agent leads

It proposes the next check with a short reason, and asks for context when the answer would change what it checks next.

The file has GPS and a capture time, so I can check the weather on that day. Shall I?
The person leads

They can take the initiative at any time, by asking a question, naming a check or adding context.

Wait, my friend said it was from last summer. Can you check who posted it?
Both sides on one board

Explanations for and against an answer helped people tell correct from incorrect AI output. One-sided ones mainly persuaded.

  • About 20 seconds.
  • The investigation is mixed-initiative. The agent proposes the next check with a short reason, and asks for context when the answer would change what it checks next. The person can take over at any time, by asking a question, naming a check or adding context.
  • Each result appears as an explained card. The evidence board collects the findings by what they point to, marks each as strong evidence or a clue, and adds a short learning tip.
  • Beat 2. Two design choices from the literature. We show both sides, because explanations for and against an answer helped people tell correct from incorrect AI output, while one-sided ones mainly persuaded. That study is a preprint. And the trust report comes once the useful checks have run or when the person asks, because showing AI advice only on request reduced overreliance.
