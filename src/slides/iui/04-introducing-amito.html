  • About 20 seconds.
  • AMITO is the companion chatbot of STOP&SCAN, a metacognitive framework that helps non-experts calibrate their decisions to the evidence. The web chat is its main interface, and Telegram and Discord bots offer the same agent.
  • There are two ways in. Upload an image or a video, and AMITO reads the file itself.
  • Beat 2. Or paste a post link from Instagram, X, Bluesky, Pinterest, Flickr or Imgur. AMITO fetches the image, the caption and the posting account.
  • Beat 3. The chat follows the five elements of STOP&SCAN. At STOP, AMITO asks what made the person stop before it checks anything. Source, Content and Alignment hold the checks, on provenance, pixels and context. At Now Reflect the person weighs the evidence, and unresolved is a legitimate outcome.
