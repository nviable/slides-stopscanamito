Source. AMITO web chat, screen recording, Oct 2026.

  • About 1 minute 40 seconds, most of it the recording. Click the video to pause it.
  • STOP. AMITO asks what made the person stop before it checks anything. The answer is a story. A friend took it yesterday in New York.
  • Because the answer names a place, the agent starts with context. The landmark and the camera's GPS both put the photo in Malmö, Sweden. AMITO resolves "yesterday" against today and names the discrepancy.
  • Then the pixels. The detectors see no AI traces, which is a clue, not proof. Then provenance. No Content Credentials, which proves nothing either way. Then the weather. The records for that place and time say cloudy, the photo shows a clear sky.
  • Each result appears as an explained card, and the evidence board fills up by what each finding points to.
  • Beat 2. The trust report comes only when the person asks. Misleading context, high confidence. A real photo with a misleading story. It is a reference verdict, and the person decides.
