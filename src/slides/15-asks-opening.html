However, the usability gap remains.

Camera Nikon paused its camera credentials

Nikon suspended content credentials on its only C2PA camera, the Z6III, in Sep 2025 after a signing flaw, and invalidated every certificate it had issued.

DPReview, Sep 2025
Phone Pixel signatures forged on rooted phones

A researcher forged Pixel Camera signatures on rooted or fault-injected Pixel 8a and 9a phones, without extracting the key.

Buchanan, Aug 2026 · blog, not peer-reviewed
Research A photographed fake still gets a valid signature

Park et al., USENIX Security 2025
