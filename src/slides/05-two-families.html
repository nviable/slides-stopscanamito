Detection and provenance

Two families of evidence

DetectionInfers from the content itself. Returns a signal, not a verdict.
ProvenanceAttests to history. Says who signed what and when, not whether the claim is true.

Apple Reference Image is signed at the sensor and sits beside C2PA. A reference image can be referenced from a C2PA manifest, and C2PA can be carried inside JPEG Trust. Complementary layers, not yet integrated.
