SIFT and lateral reading

No slot for provenance. A credential, or a missing one, has nowhere to count as evidence.

Search can backfire. Encouraging people to search raised belief in false stories when results were low quality.

Literacy tips and one-off training

Broad skepticism overshoots. Tips cut belief in false information and in true information.

One-off training fades. An hour-long session for 1,224 adults produced no average improvement.

Our expert panel · preliminary · N = 14

12 of 14 still find SIFT useful for AI content. None agreed it gives enough guidance when a trusted source posts a fabrication, and only 1 did when real content is called AI.

Government rules for marking and labelling

Aslett et al., Nature 2024 · Hoes et al., Nature Human Behaviour 2024 · Badrinathan, APSR 2021 · India IT Rules amendment, in force 20 Feb 2026 · California AI Transparency Act, operative 2 Aug 2026 · Korea AI Framework Act, in force 22 Jan 2026 · Singapore election ads amendment, in force 22 Jan 2025

  • Keep this to about a minute. SIFT and lateral reading are sound and we build on them.
  • Four gaps. No slot for provenance. Search can backfire (Aslett, Nature 2024). Broad skepticism overshoots (Hoes, NHB 2024). One-off training fades (Badrinathan, APSR 2021).
  • Expert panel, preliminary, N = 14. 12 of 14 agree SIFT is still useful for AI-generated content (R9). 0 of 14 agree it gives enough guidance when a trustworthy source publishes a fabrication (R11), and 1 of 14 when real content is wrongly called AI (R12).
  • Beat 2. The whole pile is already on stage, detection included. Everyday practice is marked, and the rules that shape inputs move below the pile. India requires a visible label and technical provenance for permitted synthetic media from 20 Feb 2026. The California AI Transparency Act requires a latent provenance disclosure from 2 Aug 2026. Korea requires a generative-output label from 22 Jan 2026, with fact-finding and penalties deferred for at least one year. Singapore prohibits realistic manipulated candidate advertising during an election period from 22 Jan 2025.
